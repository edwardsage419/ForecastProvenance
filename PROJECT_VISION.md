# Project Vision

Version: 1.0 draft
Status: PRE_GENESIS_DESIGN

## Mission

Build durable evidence infrastructure that lets independent reviewers determine what a forecast meant, what information was admissible, what method produced it, when its substantive content demonstrably existed, how the outcome was resolved, and how performance was measured.

## Strategic position

Forecast Provenance Project is infrastructure for trust in forecasts. It is not a forecasting model vendor, prediction market, high frequency signal service, or consumer forecasting application.

The project will judge methods under explicit evidence contracts. Statistical models, econometric models, language models, agentic systems, experts, consensus forecasts, and legally appropriate market implied probabilities can all be evaluated without becoming dependencies of the trust layer.

## Long term moat

The primary asset is verifiable elapsed prospective history.

That history gains value when five properties accumulate together:

1. Strong external time provenance.
2. Stable and versioned semantics.
3. Reproducible evidence and method bindings.
4. Complete accounting of failures, unresolved cases, retries, and corrections.
5. Cross method comparability under compatible information sets.

Code can be reproduced later. A genuinely prospective record with defensible external anchoring cannot be recreated years after the fact without leaving evidence problems.

## Strategic sequence

1. Forecast Trust Core.
2. Adversarial review of Trust Core.
3. Genesis Protocol and external time anchor acceptance.
4. Prospective Forecast Ledger.
5. Outcome and Evaluation Ledger.
6. Forecast Failure Corpus.
7. Model neutral trust profiles and audit outputs.
8. Low maintenance institutional audit, benchmark, data, and verification services only after evidence history and demand exist.

## Cost doctrine

The default recurring cash budget is zero.

The architecture prefers local deterministic execution, Git tracked authoritative artifacts, compact JSON, static publication, open source dependencies, and bounded CI. A database, hosted API, always on service, paid data feed, or paid model is admitted only through a recorded decision showing a demonstrated scientific or operational need.

## Non goals during the early years

1. Maximizing forecast count.
2. Training frontier models.
3. Real time data breadth.
4. High frequency forecasting.
5. Consumer SaaS features.
6. Prediction market liquidity.
7. Large forecasting communities.
8. Universal opaque trust scores.

## Predecessor relationship

Methodological influences external to this repository grant no external artifact native evidentiary status inside this project.
