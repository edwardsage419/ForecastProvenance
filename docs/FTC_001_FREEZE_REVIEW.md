## Review standard

FTC_001 can freeze when consequential trust claims have deterministic validation rules or explicitly bounded human review rules, the threat model covers known selection and provenance failure modes, and unresolved items are either nonblocking implementation details or explicitly reserved Genesis blockers.

## Third review finding disposition

### F3-B01 Cycle plan selection freedom

RESOLVED.

Confirmatory slots are deterministically generated by the IssuanceSchedulePolicy bound by the applicable trusted manifest. A cycle plan inconsistent with that policy is invalid.

### F3-B02 Randomness precomputation

RESOLVED.

Operator chosen seeds are ineligible for confirmatory stochastic methods. Eligible stochastic methods use postcommit public randomness selected by a frozen PublicRandomnessPolicy, or independently auditable complete request accounting.

### F3-B03 Outcome information barrier

RESOLVED.

TargetDefinition binds an ex ante outcome information barrier rule. Every confirmatory slot requires `external_proof_deadline <= outcome_information_barrier`.

### F3-B04 Source artifact discretion

RESOLVED.

SourceContract binds a deterministic artifact selection rule or ordered selection policy. Favorable discretionary selection is invalid.

### F3-H01 Current verifiability time semantics

RESOLVED.

CurrentVerifiabilityReport includes an explicit `as_of` timestamp and remains separate from immutable historical ValidationReport content.

### F3-H02 Governance successor forks

RESOLVED.

AcceptanceRule contains an explicit successor fork rule. Unresolved competing governance successors fail closed.

### F3-H03 Historical schedule reinterpretation

RESOLVED.

Historical cycle validation uses the exact IssuanceSchedulePolicy version and full hash bound by its historical trusted manifest.
