# Scientific Invariants

Version: 1.0 draft
Status: PRE_GENESIS_DESIGN

Any change to an invariant requires an explicit version change, a decision record, and an assessment of effects on historical comparability.

## S1 No future information

A replay, historical reconstruction, or simulated forecast may use only information defensibly available at its information cutoff.

## S2 Forecast immutability

After formal issuance, substantive forecast content is immutable. Corrections append records and preserve the original.

## S3 Versioned semantics

Any definition that can alter interpretation, forecast meaning, resolution, scoring, calibration, or numerical output requires an explicit version.

## S4 Reproducibility

A claimed result must be reproducible from retained or reproducibly referenced evidence, code, configuration, and relevant versions.

## S5 Independent resolution

Outcome rules must be fixed with enough independence from observed outcomes to prevent hindsight driven reinterpretation.

## S6 Evidence traceability

Consequential observations, transformations, forecasts, resolutions, and evaluations require a defensible evidence chain.

## S7 Observation remains observation

A proxy or measured signal cannot silently become a stronger real world construct without separate validation.

## S8 Explicit uncertainty

Unknown, unavailable, ambiguous, conflicting, and insufficient evidence states remain explicit.

## S9 No silent rewrite

Bug fixes, revised data, definition changes, improved extraction, model changes, and resolution changes must not silently replace authoritative historical outputs.

## S10 Evaluation integrity

Evaluation must account for the forecast cohort that actually existed under the accepted protocol. Failed, unresolved, excluded, and poor performing cases cannot disappear from accounting.

## S11 Genuine human evidence remains human

A protocol requirement for independent human judgment cannot be satisfied by an LLM, synthetic persona, or relabelled machine output.

## S12 Missing evidence cannot be inferred into existence

Model capability cannot convert absent evidence or unknown historical state into valid evidence.

## S13 Prospective status requires external existence evidence

A forecast is not classified as genuine prospective history until its substantive content is bound to an accepted external time anchor under the active Genesis Protocol.

## S14 Integrity and predictive skill are separate claims

The Trust Layer verifies provenance, timing, semantic bindings, lifecycle integrity, and reproducibility. Predictive skill is established only by later outcome evaluation under declared scoring rules.

## S15 Native evidence begins at this project's own Genesis

Predecessor artifacts, synthetic fixtures, retrospective experiments, and historical demonstrations cannot be represented as native prospective history.

## S16 Trust uncertainty fails closed

When a required availability time, trusted root, anchor proof, target binding, method binding, resolution binding, or other consequential trust condition cannot be verified, the stronger trust claim is ineligible.
